# clock-stamp-privacy
Privacy Policy

Clock Stamp uses your location only to show your local city and fetch weather information. Location data may be sent to weather service providers to provide current weather conditions.

Clock Stamp stores your preferences, selected cities, language, appearance, and display settings locally on your device.

We do not sell your data, use your data for advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

If you contact us by email through the app, your email address and message will be used only to respond to your support request.
